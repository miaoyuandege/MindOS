# License decision packet

Recommended: **MIT**, subject to the owner's explicit decision and confirmation of the attribution name. LICENSE is BLOCKED_BY_USER_DECISION; this document is not a license grant or legal advice.

| Option | Benefits | Obligations / trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIT | Short, permissive; commercial use, modification and redistribution are allowed | Retain the copyright and permission notice; no warranty; no express patent grant comparable to Apache-2.0 |
| Apache-2.0 | Permissive, with an explicit contributor patent grant | Preserve applicable notices and license, mark changed files, carry applicable NOTICE attributions; patent-litigation termination provision; no trademark grant |

MIT is the recommendation for this small protocol/Skill/stdlib-tool distribution because it minimizes adoption and maintenance overhead. Prefer Apache-2.0 if explicit contributor patent terms are more important than brevity. Neither grants rights the contributor does not hold. No copyleft option is added without a project goal requiring it.

Sources: [MIT terms and summary](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/), [Apache-2.0 terms](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The comparison is a practical decision aid, not legal clearance.
